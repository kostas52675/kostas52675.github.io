About Me

I’m a second-year MSc student in Cognitive Science at the National and Kapodistrian University of Athens, working where human reasoning meets machine reasoning. My background spans economics (Panteion University) and psychology (Metropolitan College), and I now build and evaluate models on language, behavioural and neural data. One question drives most of what I do: how the brain processes information, and whether we could describe that process well enough to model it on a computer.

My Projects

  • Drosophila Connectome: Neuron-Type Classification (Python, scikit-learn). Predicts the class of all 139,246 neurons in the adult fruit-fly brain from how each neuron is wired. Macro F1 0.84 against a 0.37 baseline.
  • Doomscrolling Detector (Python, YOLOv8, MediaPipe). A local webcam tool that detects when I am on my phone during a work session and interrupts me with a video alert. It combines YOLOv8 phone detection, head pitch and eye gaze from MediaPipe's Face Landmarker, and keyboard/mouse activity, with per-user calibration and a unit-tested state machine. Built with Claude Code; I have studied and maintain the codebase. View on GitHub
